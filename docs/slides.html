objective  Measure knowledge as a probability, plan the cheapest route to a goal, and justify every step with numbers.

02 · solution

A GPS does three things. So does Meje.

01 · locate

Hidden Markov model

Reads noisy answers and keeps a belief over unknown, fragile and mastered for each skill.

02 · route

A* search

Finds the cheapest order of skills through the prerequisite map, in minutes.

03 · steer

Markov decision process

Chooses a lesson, a question or moving on, by expected value.

answer + confidence→hmm: belief→mdp: next step⇄a*: next skill→eta, pass chance, reason
one goal, three algorithms chained: the belief sets the action values and every edge cost of the routelive demo →

05 · originality and practical value

Everyone has the algorithms. Nobody shows the receipts.

not different, and we say so

  • Knowledge tracing is from 1995, A* from 1968
  • Exam readiness scores exist: UWorld, Achievable
  • Duolingo, Khan and Math Academy win on content and reach

different

  • A probability, an ETA and the reason, on one screen
  • A ledger: its forecasts against what happened
  • Not a chat: no answer before an attempt
  • Runs offline, no dependencies, nothing stored on a server

next market  The UNT in Kazakh: 233,000 test-takers and several official attempts a year, so the forecast can be checked in public.

06 · limits and next steps

What is not proven yet, and what comes next

limits

  • Teaching parameters are assumed, not fitted
  • QMDP ignores the value of information
  • No forgetting, so no spaced review yet
  • Multiple choice only; graph built by hand

next

  • Fit the teaching parameters from our own logs
  • Add forgetting and spaced review
  • Pilot with classmates: publish predicted against actual
  • A Kazakh pack for the UNT
